Surfaces, Canvas & Substrates Master Guide

PREPARATION • PRIMING • VARNISHING
Working with Canvas, Cartridge Paper, Canvas Pads, Watercolor Paper, Glass, Plastic, Wood, and Terracotta.

Canvas (Stretched & Panels)

Traditional Fine Art Substrates

The timeless standard for master painters. Woven from cotton duck or linen, stretched over kiln-dried pine stretcher bars or mounted on rigid MDF board.

Available Formats & Tooth
Stretched Cotton Duck • 10oz - 12oz (300-380 GSM) • Medium Grain
Best for: Acrylics, Oil Painting, Mixed Media Impasto
Canvas Board / Panel • Rigid 3mm - 4mm MDF backing • Fine to Medium
Best for: Plein air studies, heavy palette knife work, students
Linen Canvas • Belgian / European flax • Ultra-fine to coarse
Best for: Archival portraiture, professional masterworks (highest longevity)
Medium Compatibility
Acrylic Colours (Heavy Body / Fluid / Satin) Perfect (100%)
Oil Colours Perfect (100%)
Acrylic Paint Markers (Posca, Doms) Excellent (95%)
Oil Pastels Very Good (85%)
Surface Preparation: 1. Inspect for tautness; insert corner keys if stretched canvas is sagging.
2. If raw canvas: Apply 2 coats of Acrylic Gesso with a wide flat brush in criss-cross strokes.
3. Allow 2-3 hours dry time, lightly sand with 240-grit sandpaper between coats for satin finish.
Varnishing: Once fully cured (72h for acrylics, 6-12 months for oils), apply Acrylic Gloss/Satin Varnish with a wide flat wash brush or spray can in dust-free environment.

Canvas Pads

Portable Fine Art Substrates

Glue-bound or spiral-bound pads containing real woven canvas sheets primed with acrylic gesso. Combines the authentic tooth of stretched canvas with the convenience of paper.

Available Formats & Tooth
Standard Primed Canvas Sheets • 280 - 300 GSM • Medium woven grain
Best for: Practice drills, color studies, classroom studies, easy framing in photo frames
Medium Compatibility
Acrylic Paints & Gouache 100%
Oil Paint Studies 95%
Pastel Pencils & Oil Pastels 90%
Surface Preparation: 1. Peel or cut sheet from pad along the glue line.
2. Tape securely on all four edges to a drawing board using low-tack painter's masking tape to prevent curling.
3. Pre-primed; no additional gesso required unless an ultra-smooth finish is preferred.
Varnishing: Allow full drying, apply spray varnish or mount onto backing board for framing behind glass.

Cartridge & Sketching Paper

Paper & Boards

Uncoated, high-quality drawing paper with subtle tooth. An artist essential for sketching, graphite, charcoal, colored pencils, brush pens, and mixed media.

Available Formats & Tooth
Lightweight Cartridge • 90 - 120 GSM • Very light tooth
Best for: Quick gesture sketches, pencil studies, dry pen sketches
Medium Weight Cartridge • 130 - 160 GSM • Medium fine tooth
Best for: Graphite pencil shading (HB-10B), colored pencils, soft pastels
Heavyweight Mixed Media Cartridge • 180 - 250 GSM • Sturdy vellum finish
Best for: Doms & Camel brush pens, acrylic marker illustrations, light watercolor & gouache
Medium Compatibility
Graphite Pencils (HB - 14B) & Charcoal 100%
Colored Pencils (Polychromos, Prismacolor) 100%
Brush Pens (Doms, Camel) & Fineliners 95%
Soft Pastels & Conte Crayons 90%
Surface Preparation: 1. Choose acid-free / archival paper to prevent yellowing over time.
2. For wet washes on 180+ GSM, tape down edges to a stiff MDF board.
3. Keep a clean sheet of scrap paper under your drawing wrist to prevent hand oil transfer.
Varnishing: Protect pencil & pastel works with 2 misting passes of Workable Art Fixative from 30cm away.

Watercolor Paper (Cold, Hot & Rough)

Paper & Boards

Heavy cotton or cellulose paper treated with gelatin/synthetic sizing to control moisture absorption, allowing luminous watercolor pigments to float and bloom.

Available Formats & Tooth
Cold Pressed (NOT finish) • 300 GSM (140 lb) • Distinct dimpled texture
Best for: Universal choice - wet-on-wet washes, blooms, dry-brushing
Hot Pressed (Smooth finish) • 300 GSM (140 lb) • Silky smooth plate
Best for: Botanical illustrations, fine ink & wash, watercolor pencils, portraits
Rough • 300 - 640 GSM (300 lb) • Deep rugged grain
Best for: Dramatic seascapes, granulating pigments, expressive loose strokes
Medium Compatibility
Watercolors (Cakes & Tubes) 100%
Gouache & Aqua Color 100%
Watercolor Pencils & Brush Pens 98%
Acrylics (Diluted washes / Glazes) 90%
Surface Preparation: 1. If paper is under 300 GSM, soak in clean water bath for 3 minutes and stretch onto a wooden drawing board using gummed tape.
2. 300 GSM glued watercolor blocks can be painted on directly without pre-stretching.
3. Use artist masking fluid on dry paper to preserve brilliant white highlights.
Varnishing: Frame behind UV-protective glass with a mat/mount; do not apply liquid varnish directly over traditional watercolors.

Wood Panels & MDF / Pine

Rigid & Dimensional Substrates

Solid birch plywood, basswood cradle panels, MDF boards, and raw pine slabs. Provides a solid, vibration-free surface that never sags under heavy paint or knife work.

Available Formats & Tooth
Cradled Birch Plywood • Solid 1/4" face + pine cradle • Ultra-smooth fine grain
Best for: Detailed oils, acrylics, resin pours, wood staining
MDF / Hardboard Panel • Dense engineered wood • Uniform flat surface
Best for: Smooth portraiture, palette knife painting, experimental panels
Raw Pine / Tree Slices • Natural live-edge log cross-sections • Natural grain ridges
Best for: Rustic acrylics, pyrography + paint, folk art
Medium Compatibility
Acrylic Paints & Gels 100%
Oil Paints 98%
Acrylic Markers (Posca, Doms) 95%
Wood Stains & Inks 95%
Surface Preparation: 1. Sand thoroughly: Start with 120-grit, progress to 220-grit along the woodgrain direction.
2. Wipe clean of all sawdust using a damp lint-free cloth or tack cloth.
3. Apply 1 coat of Sanding Sealer or PVA size to seal wood pores and prevent tannin discoloration.
Varnishing: Finish with high-gloss liquid acrylic varnish or clear epoxy resin for an ultra-deep glass-like dimension.

Glass & Mirrors

Non-Porous Substrates

Smooth, non-porous silica glass, glass jars, framed window panes, and decorative mirror surfaces for reverse glass painting and vibrant translucent crafts.

Available Formats & Tooth
Picture Frame Glass / Sheets • • Zero tooth (Ultra-slick)
Best for: Reverse glass painting, modern signage, stained glass effects
Glass Bottles & Jars • • Curved slick surface
Best for: Upcycling decor, floral vases, fairy light displays
Medium Compatibility
Acrylic Paint Markers (Posca, Doms C3) 95%
Specialized Glass Paints (Pebeo Vitrea) 100%
Acrylics mixed with Glass Medium / GAC 200 90%
Alcohol Inks 92%
Surface Preparation: 1. Deep clean glass with 99% Isopropyl Alcohol or glass cleaner to eradicate oils, grease, and fingerprints.
2. Wear latex/nitrile gloves when handling cleaned glass.
3. Avoid sanding unless a frosted effect is desired.
Varnishing: For decorative display, seal with crystal clear acrylic spray sealer (2 light mist coats). For bakeable paints, bake at 150°C (300°F) for 40 mins.

Plastic, Acrylic Sheets (Perspex) & Yupo

Non-Porous Substrates

Polycarbonate, acrylic sheets (Plexiglass), synthetic waterproof Yupo paper, and rigid plastic objects for contemporary fluid art and pop illustrations.

Available Formats & Tooth
Clear Acrylic Sheets (Perspex) • • Zero tooth
Best for: Floating glass-like artwork, layered 3D acrylic paintings
Yupo Synthetic Paper • • Silky polypropylene sheet
Best for: Alcohol inks, liquid watercolors, experimental marbling
Rigid Molded Plastics / Phone Cases • • Smooth slick polymer
Best for: Custom custom illustration, sneaker art, phone cases
Medium Compatibility
Acrylic Markers (Posca, Doms) 98%
Alcohol Inks (Piñata, Ranger) 100%
Acrylic Paint (with Plastic Primer) 90%
Surface Preparation: 1. Clean with mild dish soap and warm water. DO NOT use acetone, which melts and fogs acrylic plastics!
2. For rigid plastics to be painted with brush acrylics: Lightly scuff surface with 600-grit sandpaper for mechanical bite.
3. Spray 1 light mist of plastic primer if covering large areas.
Varnishing: Seal with water-based acrylic aerosol gloss or satin varnish. Avoid harsh solvent lacquers that craze plastic.

Terracotta, Bisque & Air-Dry Clay

Earthen & Sculptural Substrates

Porous baked red terracotta pots, unglazed ceramic bisque, and air-dry clay sculptures. Highly porous and absorbs water rapidly.

Available Formats & Tooth
Terracotta Flower Pots • • Sandy porous clay
Best for: Boho plant pot painting, mandala art, garden ornaments
Air-Dry Clay Sculptures (DAS / Fimo) • • Chalky smooth to textured
Best for: Miniature figurines, jewelry trays, decorative wall hangings
Medium Compatibility
Acrylic Paints (Mont Marte, Camel) 100%
Acrylic Paint Markers (Posca, Doms) 98%
Gouache Colours 85%
Surface Preparation: 1. Wipe dust off with a barely damp sponge and let dry 30 minutes.
2. Clay absorbs moisture fast: seal with 1 coat of diluted acrylic gesso (70% gesso + 30% water) or clear acrylic varnish primer.
3. This prevents the raw clay from draining water out of your acrylic paint too fast.
Varnishing: Coat with 2-3 layers of exterior gloss varnish if used for outdoor planters, or brush-on polyurethane.
